BizTrack

Privacy Policy

BizTrack helps small businesses track sales, expenses, customers, reminders, and reports. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Last updated: September 26, 2026

By using BizTrack, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Information we collect

Account and profile information

When you create or access an account, we may process your name, email address, profile photo, phone number, store name, business category, and authentication details. You may sign in with email and password or Google Sign-In.

Business records

BizTrack processes the information you choose to enter, such as sales, expenses, customer or supplier names, payment status, amounts, dates, notes, categories, and report data.

Images and scanned bills

If you scan or attach a bill, receipt, or other image, BizTrack processes that image to extract text and help you review business records. Images may be compressed and uploaded to ImageBB when the app stores an image online.

Device and app information

The app may store preferences, pending offline transactions, notification history, and reminder settings on your device. BizTrack also receives basic technical information needed to provide authentication, cloud storage, synchronization, and error handling.

2. How we use information

  • Provide account creation, sign-in, profile, and sign-out functions.
  • Save, synchronize, and display your business records across supported devices.
  • Process bill images with on-device OCR and, when needed, cloud OCR or AI services.
  • Generate summaries and PDF reports that you request.
  • Schedule local reminders and notifications for payments, dues, and other events you configure.
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Services we use

BizTrack relies on service providers to operate parts of the app. Depending on the feature and your device, information may be processed by:

  • Firebase for authentication, cloud database storage, and app infrastructure.
  • Google Sign-In when you choose Google authentication.
  • ImageBB for hosting uploaded bill or receipt images.
  • OCR.space and Groq when cloud OCR or AI-assisted bill parsing is required.

These providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Please review their policies for details about their handling of data.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We share information only as needed to provide BizTrack through the services described above, comply with applicable law, protect the security of the app and its users, or respond to a lawful request. We do not share your business records for advertising or sell them to third parties.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your cloud account and business records are retained while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. Information stored locally remains on your device until you clear the app data, uninstall the app, or remove it through an available in-app setting.

To request account and associated data deletion, contact us at support@biztrack.app. We may retain limited information where required by law, for fraud prevention, or to resolve disputes.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including authenticated access and encrypted connections to supported services, to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

BizTrack is intended for business users and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Your choices

  • You can choose whether to create an account and whether to use Google Sign-In.
  • You can manage notification permissions in your device settings.
  • You can avoid uploading images or use manual entry instead.
  • You can request access, correction, or deletion of your account information by contacting us.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Your continued use of BizTrack after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact us

For privacy questions, data requests, or account deletion requests, email support@biztrack.app.
