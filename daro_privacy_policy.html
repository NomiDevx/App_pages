Privacy Policy for Daro

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 | Last Updated: October 1, 2026

Welcome to Daro: Voice Diary & Audio Journal ("we," "our," or "us"). Your privacy is our highest priority. Daro is crafted as an authentic, private sanctuary for your thoughts, spoken reflections, mood tracking, and personal memories.

1. The Privacy Sanctuary Principle

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your voice recordings, transcripts, or personal journal entries to advertisers, data brokers, or third parties under any circumstances.

2. Information We Process

  • Audio & Voice Recordings: Audio is captured only when you press record. It is saved in your device's isolated app storage sandbox and synced with encrypted cloud storage (Supabase) if you sign in.
  • Account Credentials: If you sign up using Email or Google Sign-In, we store your email address and display name for authentication. Guest sessions require no personal details.
  • Photos: Any images attached to your entries remain strictly private.
  • Biometrics: Passcode and fingerprint locks are verified on-device via the Android OS BiometricPrompt. We never store or transmit your biometrics.
  • Advertising & Device Identifiers (Google AdMob): To support free access, Daro may display advertisements powered by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect your Google Advertising ID (AAID), device type, and technical diagnostic telemetry in compliance with Google Play Developer policies. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID anytime via Android Settings → Google → Ads.

3. Google Play Data Safety Summary

Data Category Collected Shared Purpose
Voice / Audio Recordings Yes (On-Device / Cloud) No Core Voice Diary Feature
Account Info (Email, Name) Yes (If Registered) No Account & Cloud Sync

4. Account and Data Deletion Policy

In accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, users can permanently delete their account and associated data:

  • In-App: Go to Settings → Delete Account & Data.
  • By Email: Contact us at privacy@daroapp.com with subject "Delete My Account". Your request will be executed within 30 days.

5. Children's Privacy

Daro is intended for general audiences aged 13+. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Contact Us

For questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please email support@daroapp.com.
